By this point in a long article the hand has done enough. Autoscroll keeps walking the page for you: a thousand pixels at a time, until you scroll up or click the icon again.

There is no overlay on the article. The page stays the page. The only chrome is the toolbar button you already clicked.

Steep bit into Alfriston. A pub you will not go into because the notes are not finished. Then the river, then the car park, then the sentence that finally lets you stop.

That is the behaviour: start on click, stop on scroll-up, nothing stored, nothing sent.
